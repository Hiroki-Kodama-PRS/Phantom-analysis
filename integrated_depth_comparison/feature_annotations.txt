Feature Annotations for Grouped Categories (n>1)
================================================================================

3mm_Ischaemia:
  - Time IR: Diastolic_Width, Fall_Time
  - Intensity Red: AC_Component, Amplitude, PI
  - Intensity IR: AC_Component, Amplitude, PI
  - Area Red: AUC, D_AUC, Max_Start_Datum_Diff
  - Area IR: AUC, D_AUC, End_Datum_Area, Max_Start_Datum_Diff, S_AUC, Start_Datum_Area

9mm_Ischaemia:
  - Intensity IR: AC_Component, Amplitude
  - Area IR: AUC, D_AUC, End_Datum_Area, S_AUC, Start_Datum_Area
  - Time IR: Diastolic_Width, Fall_Time

15mm_Ischaemia:
  - Intensity IR: AC_Component, Amplitude, PI
  - Area IR: D_AUC, Max_Start_Datum_Diff, Start_Datum_Area

3mm_Congestion:
  - Time IR: Diastolic_Width, Fall_Time
  - Shape & Stats IR: IPAR, RWI, Variance
  - Intensity Red&IR: AC_Component_Ratio, Amplitude_Ratio, PI_Ratio
  - Intensity IR: AC_Component, Amplitude, PI
  - Area IR: AUC, D_AUC, End_Datum_Area, Max_Start_Datum_Diff, S_AUC, Start_Datum_Area

9mm_Congestion:
  - Intensity IR: AC_Component, Amplitude
  - Time IR: Diastolic_Width, Fall_Time

15mm_Congestion:
  - Intensity IR: AC_Component, Amplitude, PI
  - Area IR: D_AUC, Max_Start_Datum_Diff, Start_Datum_Area
